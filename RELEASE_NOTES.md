New look, dark mode and one-click updates.

- **Dark mode:** the theme button in the header switches between Auto (matches your device), Light and Dark.
- **Updates:** when a new version comes out, Recipe Book asks if you want it. On Windows, "Update and restart" downloads it and reopens the app on the new version. The web app reloads instead. Your recipes, plan and list are kept.
- **Redesign:** a grocery-store look. The shopping list is grouped under numbered aisle signs, planner days look like shelf tags, and there are smooth transitions between sections.
- **Fix:** on phones, a long recipe title no longer pushes the page wider than the screen.

Note: from this version on, the desktop app can save its own update file and restart itself. Nothing else on your computer is reachable from the app.
