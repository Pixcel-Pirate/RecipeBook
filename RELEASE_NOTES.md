New look, a grocery checklist on every recipe, and seamless desktop updates.

- **New look:** a cleaner, modern design with a frosted top bar, sliding section tabs, new fonts and a violet accent, in light and dark.
- **Grocery list:** open a recipe and tap **Grocery list**. Tick each item you already have; a bar shows how many you've got. Ticks are remembered per recipe.
- **Desktop updates:** "Update and restart" now installs the whole new version quietly and reopens Recipe Book, the same way Stancebook does. Your recipes, plan and lists are kept.
